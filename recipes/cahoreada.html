Tostada Cachoreada

Home

    Ingredients - 4 tostadas

  • 4 crispy corn tostadas
  • 250 g cooked shrimp, chopped
  • 150 g white fish, cooked and chopped
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1/2 red or white onion, finely chopped
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/2 cup lime juice
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 avocado, sliced or diced
  • 1-2 jalapeños or serrano peppers, finely chopped
  • Salt and black pepper
  • hot sauce, to taste

    Preparation

  1. Prepare the seafood

    2. If you're using raw fish, cut it into small pieces and marinate it in lime juice until it becomes opaque.

    Alternatively, you can use already cooked seafood, which is easier and more predictable.

    For shrimp, cook them in boiling water for few minutes until pink and opaque. Let them cool and chop them.

  2. Prepare the vegetables

    3. Dice the tomato, cucumber onion, and jalapeño. Finely chop the cilantro.

  3. Make the seafood mixture

    4. In a large bowl, combine:

    • Shrimp
    • Fish
    • Tomato
    • Cucumber
    • Onion
    • Jalapeño
    • Cilantro

      • Add lime juice, salt, pepper, and hot sauce. Mix gently

  4. Assemble the tostadas

    5. Spread a little avocado or seafood mixture directly onto each tostada.

    Then generously add the seafood mixture on top.

    Finish with:

    • Avocado
    • A squeeze of lime
    • Hot sauce
    • A little cilantro
