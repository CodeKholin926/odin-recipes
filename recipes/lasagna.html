Preparation

  1. Prepare the meat sauce

    1. Heat a little olive oil in a large pan over medium heat
    2. Add chopped onion and cook for 3-4 minutes
    3. Add minced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds
    4. Add the ground beef and cook until completely browned
    5. Add tomato sauce, crushed tomatoes, and tomato paste.
    6. Season with salt, pepper, oregano, and italian seasoning.
    7. Let the sauce simmer for 20-30 minutes, stirring occasionally

  2. Prepare the cheese mixture

    1. In a bowl combine:
      • Ricotta cheese
      • 1 egg
      • Parmesan cheese
      • A little salt and pepper
    2. Mixed everything until well combined

  3. Prepare the noodles

    1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions
    2. Once they're done, drain them and set them aside. If you're using oven-ready/no-boil noodles, you can skip this step

  4. Assemble the Lasagna

      5. Preheat your oven to 375F (190C)

      In a baking dish:

    1. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom
    2. Add a a layer of lasagna noodles
    3. Add some of the ricotta mixture
    4. Add mozzarella cheese
    5. Add another layer of meat sauce
    6. Repeat the layers until you've used your ingredients

      7. Finish with meat sauce, mozarella, and parmesan on top

  5. Bake

    1. Cover the dish with aluminum foil and bake for approximately 25-30 minutes
    2. Remove the foil and bake for another 10-15 minutes, until the cheese on top is melted and slightly golden

  6. Let it rest

    1. Take the lasagna out of the oven and let it rest for 10-15 minutes before cutting it

      2. This allows the layers to set, so your lasagna won't fall apart when you serve it
